The article provides photographic evidence showing that real corners do not exhibit the uniform darkening SSAO produces, and notes that SSAO is a screen-space post-processing effect, not a physically based simulation.

**Background**: Ambient occlusion (AO) approximates how ambient light is blocked by nearby geometry, adding depth to 3D scenes. SSAO, introduced by Crytek in 2007 with Crysis, computes AO as a screen-space post-process, making it fast but prone to artifacts and inaccuracies.
