## [Supercooled Kidneys Transplanted in Pigs: A Landmark Achievement](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/07/23/1140765/supercooled-kidneys-have-been-transplanted-into-pigs-in-a-landmark-achievement/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have successfully transplanted supercooled kidneys into pigs, demonstrating that organs preserved at subzero temperatures without freezing can remain viable for extended periods. This breakthrough could dramatically extend the time available for organ transport and transplantation. Currently, kidneys can only be preserved for about 24-36 hours, limiting organ sharing and increasing waste. Extending preservation time via supercooling could save thousands of lives by enabling better matching, longer transport distances, and more elective surgeries. The supercooling technique involves cooling the organ to -4°C to -10°C while preventing ice formation using cryoprotective agents. In the pig study, supercooled kidneys were preserved for up to 72 hours before transplantation and functioned normally after transplant.

rss · MIT Technology Review · Jul 23, 16:58

**Background**: Organ preservation is critical for transplantation; currently, kidneys are stored on ice at around 4°C, limiting viability to about 24-36 hours. Supercooling goes below freezing without ice formation, which can slow metabolism further and extend preservation time. This approach has previously been tested in rat livers but is now shown to work in a large animal model, a key step toward human trials.
