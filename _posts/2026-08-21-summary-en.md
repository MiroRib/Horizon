## [Researcher Accidentally Hijacks ENUM, Logs Calls to Military Bases](https://lina.sh/blog/hijacking-e164-arpa) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A security researcher accidentally hijacked e164.arpa ENUM queries, logging hundreds of thousands of phone calls to military bases. The incident exposed a critical flaw in the public ENUM infrastructure. This highlights a significant vulnerability in the global telephony routing infrastructure, potentially allowing unauthorized parties to intercept call routing information. It underscores the need for better security measures in ENUM deployments and raises concerns about privacy and national security. The researcher inadvertently took control of a portion of the e164.arpa domain, which is used for ENUM lookups. The incident involved logging call routing queries for military numbers, but no actual call content was intercepted. The researcher reported the issue to authorities but faced no legal repercussions.

hackernews · gavide · Aug 21, 13:11 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49387570)

**Background**: ENUM (E.164 Number Mapping) is a protocol that uses the Domain Name System (DNS) to map telephone numbers to internet addresses, such as SIP URIs, for call routing. The e164.arpa domain is reserved for public ENUM entries, and queries are typically made by carriers and VoIP providers. DNS hijacking, the technique involved, is a type of attack where DNS queries are redirected to an attacker-controlled server, potentially exposing sensitive routing information.

## [OVOMIND Hands-On: Real-Time Emotion Sensing from Biosignals](https://www.4gamer.net/games/999/G999902/20260818017/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A hands-on report from TGS 2026 details OVOMIND, a Swiss device that estimates emotions from biosignals like heart rate and skin conductance in about 300 milliseconds. The report highlights its potential across gaming, automotive, learning, mental health, music, and conversational AI. This device could transform human-computer interaction by enabling real-time emotional feedback, leading to more adaptive and personalized experiences in gaming and beyond. It represents a step toward commercializing emotion recognition technology, which has broad implications for industries focused on user engagement and well-being. OVOMIND uses AI to convert biological signals into emotional data, and its DK1 development kit is designed for game developers to integrate emotion-driven insights. The device is a smart band that captures physiological reactions through built-in sensors, enabling real-time emotion estimation.
