## [Arm and IBM Create Dual-Architecture Chip for Enterprise](https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/processors/two-architectures-on-one-chip-arm-and-ibm-team-up-in-a-legendary-crossover-but-alas-its-for-enterprise-infrastructure/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

IBM and Arm have collaborated on a new chip that natively executes both Arm and IBM's z/Architecture instruction sets within the same core. The chip, unveiled at Hot Chips 2026, operates at over 5.7 GHz and is built on a 2nm process node. This collaboration marks a significant shift in enterprise infrastructure, offering more flexibility and choice by allowing a single chip to run both Arm-based Linux and IBM's z/OS workloads. It could reduce hardware costs and simplify data center operations, potentially influencing future processor designs. The chip features 11 high-performance cores on a 2nm die, with each core able to switch between the two instruction sets in the nanosecond range. The collaboration initially focused on virtualization and software compatibility, but now extends to hardware-level dual-ISA execution.

rss · PC Gamer · Aug 25, 12:12

**Background**: IBM's mainframe processors traditionally use the proprietary z/Architecture, while Arm is widely used in mobile and increasingly in servers. Combining both on a single chip allows enterprises to consolidate diverse workloads onto one platform, leveraging the strengths of each architecture. The chip is built on a 2nm node, which is more advanced than current consumer CPUs.
