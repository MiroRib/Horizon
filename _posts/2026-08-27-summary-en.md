## [Tailcat: netcat over Tailscale's data plane](https://github.com/tailscale/tailcat) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tailscale has released Tailcat, a netcat-like tool that uses Tailscale's encrypted data plane (magicsock) to establish peer-to-peer TCP/UDP connections without Tailscale's control plane. It enables simple, secure networking between machines without public IPs. Tailcat simplifies secure peer-to-peer networking, potentially enabling new applications and workflows that require direct connections without public IPs. It extends Tailscale's data plane to arbitrary TCP/UDP traffic, which could foster innovation in distributed systems and remote access. Tailcat uses Tailscale's magicsock for point-to-point WireGuard-encrypted tunnels, with DERP as a relay fallback if NAT traversal fails. It does not use Tailscale's control plane, meaning it operates independently of the coordination service.

hackernews · nderjung · Aug 26, 17:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49452990)

**Background**: netcat is a classic networking utility for reading and writing data over TCP/UDP connections. Tailscale is a VPN service that creates secure mesh networks using WireGuard, with a control plane for coordination and a data plane for encrypted traffic. Tailcat combines these concepts, offering a netcat-like experience over Tailscale's data plane.

This milestone was reported on their press page, marking a significant achievement for the company. Actinide's flagship commercial product is enriched ytterbium-176, a stable isotope used as a neutron capture target to produce lutetium-177 for targeted radioligand therapies. The company's calutron technology is based on 1940s mass spectrometer designs, upgraded with modern control systems and electromagnets, potentially reducing capital costs from hundreds of millions to a few hundred thousand dollars.

Calutron, short for California University Cyclotron, is a mass spectrometer developed during the Manhattan Project for isotope separation, but was later abandoned in favor of gaseous diffusion. Actinide's use of modernized calutrons represents a revival of this old technology for contemporary nuclear fuel production.
