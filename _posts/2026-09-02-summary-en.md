This article helps developers and researchers understand the trade-offs between different sampling methods, enabling better quality in graphics applications. The article covers the Bridson algorithm, which uses an active list and annulus sampling to efficiently generate Poisson disk distributions. The classical approach involves rejection sampling, while modern algorithms like Bridson's are more efficient.
