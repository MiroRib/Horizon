## [Isar Aerospace reaches orbit and deploys payloads on second flight](https://isaraerospace.com/press/history-for-european-spaceflight-isar-aerospace-reaches-orbit-and-deploys-payloads-on-second-flight) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Isar Aerospace successfully launched its two-stage Spectrum rocket into low Earth orbit from a Norwegian spaceport on its second attempt, deploying payloads. This marks the first entirely commercial orbital rocket launch from Europe. This achievement provides Europe with sovereign commercial access to space, reducing reliance on Arianespace and foreign launch providers. It also demonstrates that European startups can compete with established players like SpaceX, potentially reshaping the European launch market. The Spectrum rocket is a two-stage vehicle designed for small to medium payloads. The successful flight follows a first attempt in March that ended in failure 30 seconds after liftoff, when the vehicle crashed into the sea and exploded.
