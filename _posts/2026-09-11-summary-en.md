The statement followed OpenAI's claim that its agents solved a Millennium Prize Problem, which sparked outrage among top mathematicians and led OpenAI to withdraw sponsorship of a CalTech math event. This is a rare collective intervention by the world's most decorated mathematicians, signaling that AI's rapid incursion into mathematical research is straining credit assignment, verification, and the community's shared understanding. It could reshape how AI labs collaborate with academia and how mathematical contributions are evaluated in the AI era. The declaration frames the goals of AI companies and the mathematical community as "severely misaligned," and it follows OpenAI's August 2026 announcement that its Astra model produced ten new results on long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science. The dispute escalated when OpenAI withdrew its sponsorship of a CalTech math event after criticism from university researchers.

## [RTK's claimed token savings fail real-world cost benchmarks](https://quesma.com/blog/does-rtk-make-ai-coding-cheaper/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A critical benchmark analysis from Quesma argues that RTK (Rust Token Killer), a CLI proxy that filters and compresses terminal output before AI agents read it, does not deliver the cost savings it claims. The article contrasts RTK's reported token reductions with independent cost-per-attempt measurements, and community commenters point to specific measurement flaws such as RTK ignoring shell pipelines like `tail -5`. RTK has become one of the most popular AI coding cost-optimization tools, with over 79k GitHub stars and claims of up to 60% token reduction, so evidence that its savings are illusory could mislead a large number of developers optimizing LLM spend. The debate also highlights the broader need for independent, standardized benchmarks of AI coding tools rather than vendor-reported metrics. RTK works by rewriting shell commands into rtk equivalents and compressing bash output across 16 supported AI coding tools, but critics note that when an agent runs a command like `rtk command | tail -5`, RTK still reports the full token count as saved even though the pipeline already limited output to a few lines. RTK's own savings page claims an 89% average noise reduction measured across 2,900+ real developer sessions, while JetBrains's SkillsBench reportedly found no savings.

hackernews · michalwarda · Sep 11, 11:15 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49656471)

**Background**: AI coding agents such as Claude Code read terminal output as part of their context, and every token in that context costs money, so tools like RTK aim to cut costs by stripping noisy command output before the model sees it. RTK is a CLI proxy that intercepts and rewrites shell commands, and it has spread widely through social media claims of dramatic token reductions. Independent benchmarking of such tools is difficult because savings depend heavily on the specific commands, pipelines, and agent behaviors involved.
